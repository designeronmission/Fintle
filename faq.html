Payments

They can pay via credit card, bank transfer, or digital wallets using secure payment links. We support all major payment methods including Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

We support all major credit cards, bank transfers, PayPal, and popular digital wallets. Payment methods available may vary by country. You can see the complete list for your region in your account settings.

Most credit card payments are deposited to your account within 2 business days. Bank transfers may take 3-5 business days. You can enable Instant Payouts (where available) for a small fee to receive funds within minutes.

Taxes
