Invoicing

Creating an invoice in Fintle takes only a few moments. Enter your client's details, add line items with descriptions and amounts, apply any applicable taxes or discounts, and send it directly via email or a secure payment link. You can also save frequently used invoice formats as templates to streamline repetitive billing and maintain consistency across your records.

Yes. Fintle allows you to automate repeat billing for subscription-based services, retainer agreements, or regular clients. You can configure invoices to recur on a daily, weekly, monthly, or annual basis. The system will automatically generate and dispatch each invoice according to your schedule, and you can enable automatic payment reminders to reduce late payments.

Yes. Fintle supports invoicing in over 120 currencies, making it ideal for businesses serving international clients. Exchange rates are updated hourly to ensure accurate conversions. You can save preferred currencies per client, so repeat invoices are generated with the correct currency automatically, eliminating manual adjustments.

Absolutely. Fintle offers a range of professionally designed invoice templates, or you can create your own using our intuitive drag-and-drop editor. Add your company logo, adjust colors, and customize fields to align with your brand identity. Once designed, templates can be saved as defaults for all future invoices, ensuring a consistent and professional presentation.

Payments

Customers can pay through a variety of secure methods, including credit and debit cards, bank transfers, and popular digital wallets. Fintle supports Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Every transaction is encrypted and processed in compliance with PCI-DSS standards, giving you and your customers complete peace of mind.

Fintle supports all major credit and debit cards, bank transfers, PayPal, and widely used digital wallets. The availability of specific payment methods may vary depending on your region and your customers' locations. You can view the complete list of supported methods for your country in your account settings under Payment Preferences.

Most credit card payments are deposited into your account within 2 business days. Bank transfers typically take 3 to 5 business days to clear. For businesses that need faster access to funds, Instant Payouts are available in select regions for a nominal fee, allowing you to receive payments within minutes.

Expenses

Tracking expenses in Fintle is simple and efficient. Upload receipts through the mobile app or web portal, and our AI-powered system will automatically extract key details and categorize each expense. You can also set up recurring expenses for predictable costs. All entries are reconciled against bank statements, ensuring your records remain accurate and audit-ready at all times.

Yes. Fintle allows you to track vendor bills, schedule payments, and avoid late fees with automated reminders. You can configure multi-level approval workflows for team expenses and maintain a complete, searchable history of all vendor transactions. This ensures transparency, accountability, and timely payments across your organization.

Yes. Fintle includes a full purchase order management system. You can create purchase orders, convert them to bills once goods or services are received, and manage inventory seamlessly. The PO system helps you track orders, enforce budget controls, and maintain proper documentation for procurement and auditing purposes.

Taxes

Yes. Fintle automatically applies the correct GST, VAT, or sales tax rates based on your location and transaction details, and generates fully compliant invoices. The system is regularly updated to reflect the latest tax regulations and can handle complex scenarios such as reverse charges, exempt transactions, and inter-state supplies with ease.

Yes. Fintle integrates with official tax portals for direct filing where applicable. We currently support direct GST filing in several countries and continue to expand our integrations. For jurisdictions where direct filing is not yet available, Fintle provides exportable, filing-ready tax reports that you or your accountant can submit easily.

Fintle generates comprehensive tax reports covering sales tax, VAT, GST, and income tax summaries. You can customize reports by period, tax type, or business entity, and export them in multiple formats for your accountant or tax authority. Scheduled email delivery is also available, ensuring you never miss a reporting deadline.
