1. Scope and Meaning

This policy explains how [[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]] uses cookies and similar technologies on Fintle's website and web portal. A cookie is a small browser file. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, pixels and identifiers used by embedded services. Mobile app storage and software development kits may perform related functions; their personal information handling is also covered by our Privacy Policy.

2. Categories and Purposes

Essential technologies support requested functions such as sign-in, account security, session continuity and remembering privacy choices. Blocking them may prevent those functions from working.

Optional preference technologies remember choices beyond what is necessary to provide a requested function. Optional analytics help measure use and identify improvements. Advertising technologies, if deployed, support advertising measurement or personalisation. Only the technologies identified in the inventory below are authorised for deployment under this policy.

4. Your Choices

Optional technologies are disabled until you choose the relevant category. You can accept, reject or manage optional categories through [[COOKIE SETTINGS LINK]]. You may change your choice later through the same control. Rejecting optional technologies does not prevent access to core accounting and invoicing services.

Browser settings can also delete or restrict stored information. Deleting cookies may remove a saved preference and require you to sign in again. Different browsers and devices may need separate choices. Your browser's settings do not necessarily control identifiers inside a mobile app.

Third-party sign-in or embedded services may involve their own technologies when you choose to use them. We explain the relevant purpose and obtain any required choice before optional tracking begins.
