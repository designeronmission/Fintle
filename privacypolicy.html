1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

Fintle is operated by Aparajayah Technologies, with its registered office at Flat No.2 & 3 II Floor VOC Street, Indian Bank Colony, Madurai, Tamil Nadu - 625014. ("Fintle", "we", "us" or "our"). This policy explains how we collect, use, share, protect and retain personal information when you visit our website, register for an account, use our accounting and invoicing services, or contact us.

It applies to beta.fintle.com, www.fintle.com when launched, our associated Fintle web portal, and our official iOS and Android apps. "You" includes visitors, account holders, authorised business users and people whose information is included in records submitted to Fintle.

For registration, our own billing, security and customer support, we decide why and how personal information is used. When a business stores its customers', suppliers' or employees' information in Fintle, we generally process that information on the business's instructions. That business is responsible for explaining its own collection and use. Our Business Data Processing Addendum governs that processing where it forms part of the business's agreement with us.

2. Information We Collect and Why

The information depends on your sign-up method, the features you use and the records you provide. Required fields are identified when information is requested.

Information Purpose
Name, email address, mobile number, account identifiers and verification information Create and authenticate your account, recover access and send essential service messages.
Business name, address, contact details, GSTIN or PAN where entered, and authorised user details Set up your business profile and prepare the records and invoices you request.
Invoice details, customer and supplier contacts, ledger entries, transaction amounts, bank details and documents you enter or upload Provide accounting, invoicing, record storage and the reports supported by your plan.
Subscription details, billing address, tax details, payment references, payment status and refund records where relevant Administer your purchases from Fintle, reconcile payments and meet our own recordkeeping duties.
IP address, device and browser information, app version, access times, security events and diagnostic information Protect accounts, investigate errors and maintain service reliability.
Support messages, attachments, feedback and records of your privacy or marketing choices Respond to you, resolve issues and honour your preferences.

When you use Google sign-up, Google provides the information covered by the permissions you approve, such as an account identifier, name and email address. Any additional information must be shown in the permission request. We do not receive your Google password. Google sign-up does not by itself authorise us to read your Gmail, Drive or contacts. Email and phone sign-up use the details and verification steps shown in the registration flow.

Device permissions are requested only for enabled features that need them. For example, an attachment feature may request access to a selected file or the camera. The permission prompt explains the purpose. You can manage permissions in your device settings; denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Please do not include unnecessary personal information in invoices, attachments or support messages. Do not send us banking passwords, card security codes, UPI PINs or unrelated identity documents.

3. How We Use Information and Obtain Choices

We use information for the purposes described above, to administer your relationship with Fintle, prevent misuse, respond to lawful requests and comply with applicable obligations. Where consent is required, we request it for a stated purpose before the relevant processing. Where law permits processing without consent, we use only the applicable legal ground.

You may decline optional information or withdraw consent by contacting support@aparajayah.com. If information is necessary for a requested feature, we will explain how refusal or withdrawal affects that feature. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing or prevent retention required by law.

Promotional communications require a separate choice where applicable. You can unsubscribe through the message. Essential account, security and billing messages may continue while relevant to your service. Accepting our Terms of Use is not consent to optional marketing or optional tracking.

4. Business Records and Other People

When you provide another person's information, you must have an appropriate lawful basis and provide any required notice or permission. You control the users, accountants, advisers and recipients whom you authorise to access or receive your business records.

If your information appears in a Fintle customer's invoice or accounting record, contact that business first for matters it controls. You may also contact support@aparajayah.com. We will assist or route the request appropriately and remain responsible for processing that we control ourselves.

5. When Information Is Shared

We share relevant information with providers that support hosting, database management, authentication, communications, payment processing where enabled, support and service security. AWS provides infrastructure services and MongoDB Atlas provides managed database services. Google participates when you choose Google sign-up. The current provider details and processing locations are at Aparajayah.

Providers acting on our behalf may process information only for contracted purposes and subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. Some providers, such as payment or sign-in services, also have their own responsibilities and privacy notices.

We share records with people or services you direct us to, including authorised workspace users and invoice recipients. We may disclose information to advisers under confidentiality obligations, to comply with a binding legal requirement, or to establish or defend legal rights. We assess each request and limit disclosure to what is necessary and legally permitted.

If our business is reorganised or transferred, relevant information may be transferred with appropriate safeguards and notice where required. This does not remove your applicable rights.

6. Where Information Is Processed

Our primary application and database hosting locations are India (AWS Mumbai region and the corresponding MongoDB Atlas India region, where configured). Backup and disaster recovery locations are as configured by our infrastructure and service providers. Our providers and authorised support teams may process information in India and other countries where our service providers operate, as described in the provider list.

Where information is transferred outside India, we apply the protections and transfer conditions required by applicable law, including any applicable country restrictions. Using an Indian hosting region alone does not mean that every provider, support function, or content delivery operation processes information only in India.

7. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure and loss. Access is limited according to role and legitimate operational need.

No service can guarantee absolute security. If a personal information incident occurs, we will investigate, take appropriate corrective action and notify affected people and authorities as required by applicable law. Report suspected misuse to support@aparajayah.com without sending passwords or unnecessary confidential records.

8. Retention

We retain information for the purpose for which it was collected and for any further period required or permitted by applicable law. Retention depends on the record type, account status, customer instructions, legal requirements and any specific dispute or investigation.

Account and customer workspace information follows 30 days after account closure or deletion request, unless a longer period is required or permitted by applicable law or a customer agreement. Routine backup copies expire under 90-day rolling backup retention period. Our own billing records, required security records and legally preserved material follow category-specific retention periods based on applicable tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and legal requirements.

Account deletion does not require us to erase records that must legally be retained. Such records remain protected, are restricted to the permitted purpose, and are deleted or anonymised when that purpose and required retention end. A customer's tax recordkeeping duty does not automatically require Fintle to keep every closed workspace indefinitely.

9. Your Requests and Complaints

Contact support@aparajayah.com or use Aparajayah to request access to your information, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent. We will handle the request according to the rights and exceptions applicable to you. Where applicable law provides a nomination right, you may nominate another person to exercise your rights following death or incapacity through the same channel.

We may take proportionate steps to verify identity and authority. We will explain any lawful refusal or restriction and the available escalation route. Our Account and Data Deletion Policy explains account closure, shared records and retained data.

We will address privacy grievances expeditiously and within one month of receipt, or any shorter period required by law. Where the relevant provisions are in force and applicable, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India after using the required internal grievance process.

10. Children and Policy Updates

Fintle accounts are intended for people aged 18 or above. We do not knowingly permit children to create accounts. If you believe a child has registered, contact support@aparajayah.com. A business that includes information about children in its records must meet the applicable legal requirements and give us lawful processing instructions.

We may update this policy to reflect service or legal changes. We will revise the date and provide an appropriate notice of material changes. Where a new use requires consent, we will request it before starting that use. Continued use alone does not replace legally required consent.
