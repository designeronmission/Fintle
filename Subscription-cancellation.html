1. Scope and Purchase Information

This policy applies to paid Fintle services sold by Aparajayah Technologies. Before you pay, the checkout or order shows the service, price, applicable taxes, billing period, renewal terms and any trial or special refund offer. A more favourable specific offer and mandatory legal rights take priority over this policy.

2. Trials and Renewals

A free trial lasts for the period shown when you register. It converts to a paid plan only where that arrangement has been clearly disclosed and authorised. We disclose the charge and renewal frequency before taking a recurring payment.

3. Cancellation and Access

Cancelling stops future renewals. Unless you request earlier account deletion, access continues until the end of the period already paid for. Cancellation alone does not erase accounting records or close your account. We provide confirmation and the effective cancellation date.

Uninstalling an app or stopping its use does not cancel a subscription. Account deletion may require separate action to stop an Apple or Google subscription; we explain the relevant steps during deletion. For subscriptions billed directly by Fintle, a verified deletion request also stops future direct renewals.

4. Refund Eligibility

Subject to law and any advertised offer, subscription fees are not automatically refunded solely because you change your mind, use the service less than expected or cancel partway through a billing period.

We assess refunds for duplicate charges, incorrect charges, a renewal taken after an effective cancellation, or a material failure to provide the paid service as agreed. Where law entitles you to a refund or other remedy, we will provide it. We may offer a correction or alternative remedy only where appropriate and permitted.

If Fintle ends a paid service for its own convenience before the prepaid term expires, we refund the unused portion proportionately, unless a more favourable remedy applies. If a trial, promotion or plan has a specific money-back promise, the disclosed promise applies.

Upgrades, downgrades and additional purchases follow the price and effective date shown before confirmation. We disclose whether there is a credit or proportionate charge. A billing complaint does not require you to give up statutory rights.

5. Requesting and Receiving a Refund

Send your registered contact details, transaction reference, payment date, amount and reason to support@aparajayah.com. Do not send full card details, banking passwords, OTPs or UPI PINs.

We acknowledge the request within 30 days and communicate a decision within 40 days, subject to any shorter legal deadline. Approved refunds are initiated within 30 days to the original payment method unless another lawful method is agreed. Bank or payment provider posting times can vary; we will provide a reference where available.

For Apple or Google purchases, use the relevant store's refund process where required. We will assist with purchase information and remain responsible for remedies that law places on us.

6. Billing Complaints

Contact support@aparajayah.com if a billing concern remains unresolved. Consumer complaints are acknowledged within 48 hours and redressed within one month, or sooner where required. This policy does not prevent access to a competent consumer commission or another available remedy.
