2. Encryption Wording

Use "end-to-end encrypted" only for a defined flow in which plaintext and decryption keys are unavailable to Fintle and its service providers between the authorised endpoints. The names of cloud services, HTTPS and disk encryption do not establish that property.

If the application decrypts records on the server to calculate totals, search entries or generate reports, describe that access accurately. A possible statement, only after verification, is: "We encrypt information in transit and at rest. Authorised systems and personnel may access information when necessary to provide and support the service, subject to access controls."

If only selected fields or features have true end-to-end protection, identify those precisely and explain any recovery or sharing limitations. Avoid unverified claims of zero access, 100 percent security, universal India-only storage or compliance certification.

3. Legal Timing and Review

As of 23 September 2026, the DPDP framework has staged commencement. Do not describe all substantive duties or statutory complaint rights as already operational. The final Rules defer major operational provisions for 18 months from publication; the implementation programme should plan for the May 2027 transition and recheck the applicable commencement notifications before launch. Consent Manager provisions have a separate one-year phase. Existing applicable IT and sensitive-data obligations must still be assessed. [1–3]

The September 2026 e-commerce amendments are stated to commence on 1 January 2027. Counsel should determine Fintle's role and the provisions applicable to direct SaaS sales. Prepare for relevant changes such as complaint copies, NCH integration, discount disclosures and dark-pattern controls; do not treat Fintle's customers' businesses as a marketplace operated by Fintle merely because they issue invoices. [4–5]

Use a named Grievance Officer and operational complaint handling. Preserve non-waivable consumer rights. Do not use blanket "no refunds in any circumstances", unrestricted data-use rights or clauses that claim all losses are the user's responsibility. [3–5]

The incident process must distinguish CERT-In reporting from notices to customers and future DPDP notifications. CERT-In's 2022 directions include reporting specified incidents within six hours and maintaining ICT logs for a rolling 180 days within India. Assess applicable categories and implement the process; a privacy policy alone does not achieve this. [6]

Review retention against each applicable statute and contract. Customer tax retention duties and Fintle's own duties are different. Review the final DPDP Rules' security and processing retention provisions before they commence, including the one-year requirements in Rules 6 and 8. Do not promise unconditional immediate erasure of all copies. [1]

4. Product and Mobile Implementation

Provide working account deletion within the iOS and Android experiences and a public deletion request page. Apple requires an in-app initiation path for apps supporting account creation; Google Play also requires an external request route. A static policy page without a functioning request mechanism is insufficient. Align store privacy disclosures with actual SDK behaviour and retained data. [7–8]

Because Fintle offers Google sign-up, review Apple guideline 4.8 before deferring Apple sign-in. Unless an exception applies, the guideline requires an equivalent login option with the specified privacy features. Ordinary email or phone sign-up should not be assumed to satisfy it. Do not describe Sign in with Apple as available before implementation. [9]

Show the Privacy Policy and Terms before registration. Record the accepted terms version and the notice and consent evidence where applicable. Keep optional marketing and analytics choices separate and unticked. Provide withdrawal controls that are as easy to use as the corresponding opt-in.

Build concise notices at data collection points, especially registration, financial document uploads, optional integrations and any future external AI processing. Prepare the notice language choices required when the relevant DPDP provisions commence. Existing users may need a direct transition notice, not just an updated footer link. [1–2]

An internal security policy, incident response procedure, retention schedule and vendor agreements are also needed. They should reflect the actual implementation rather than reproduce the full EC2, VPC, NAT, SSM or deployment architecture in a public policy.

6. Suggested Collection Notices

Registration notice: "Aparajayah Technologies uses your name, email address or mobile number, account verification information and sign-in details to create and secure your Fintle account and send essential service messages. Google sign-up shares the fields shown in its permission request. See our Privacy Policy for providers, retention and your choices. Contact support@fintle.com to withdraw consent or make a request."

Terms acceptance: "I agree to the Terms of Use." Link the Privacy Policy next to the notice; do not use acceptance of it as permission for unrelated processing. Where consent is the applicable basis for account processing, provide a separate, recorded affirmative choice tied to the stated notice.

Cookie banner, after implementation: "We use essential technologies to keep Fintle working securely. With your permission, we also use the optional technologies described in our Cookie Policy." Buttons: "Accept optional", "Reject optional" and "Manage settings". If no optional technologies exist, remove the optional consent claim.

Financial upload notice, where the feature is enabled: "Fintle will process the account details, transaction descriptions, amounts and dates in this file to create your requested accounting records. Upload only information you are authorised to use."

These short notices must be adapted to the exact fields, purposes and live features. They supplement a complete privacy notice; they are not blanket authorisation for future features.

  7. Apple account deletion requirements.
  8. Google Play account deletion requirements.
  9. Apple App Review Guidelines, especially section 4.8.
