1. Parties, Scope and Roles

This Addendum forms part of the agreement between [[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]] ("Fintle") and the business customer identified in the accepted order or agreement ("Customer") when expressly incorporated there. It covers personal information Fintle processes on the Customer's behalf in providing accounting and invoicing services.

The Customer determines the purposes and permitted use of its business records. Fintle processes those records on documented instructions. These roles correspond to Data Fiduciary and Data Processor where those terms apply under Indian law. Fintle separately determines processing for its own account administration, billing, security and legal obligations, as explained in its Privacy Policy.

3. Instructions and Customer Duties

The agreement, configured settings and authorised service requests are the Customer's instructions. The Customer is responsible for the lawful collection of information, required notices and permissions, the accuracy of instructions and authorised user management.

Fintle will not use Customer record contents for unrelated advertising, sale or training general-purpose models under this Addendum. Any additional purpose requires a separate lawful arrangement and any required notice or consent. Fintle will notify the Customer if it reasonably considers an instruction unlawful and may pause the affected processing while it is resolved.

4. Confidentiality and Safeguards

Fintle will restrict access to authorised personnel with confidentiality obligations and use appropriate safeguards for the processing risk. The agreed safeguards are [[SECURITY SCHEDULE OR VERIFIED CONTROL DESCRIPTION]], covering encryption, access control, workspace separation, monitoring, backup recovery and secure deletion as applicable.

The Customer must secure its own devices, credentials, configuration and integrations. Neither party's responsibilities excuse the other's obligations.

5. Service Providers and Locations

The Customer authorises the providers identified in [[APPROVED SUBPROCESSOR LIST]]. Fintle will impose appropriate written confidentiality, security and processing obligations on providers acting on its behalf and remain responsible for their performance as required by the agreement and law.

Fintle will provide at least [[SUBPROCESSOR CHANGE NOTICE PERIOD]] notice before adding or replacing a provider that will process Customer personal information, except where an urgent lawful or security need requires a shorter period. The Customer may raise a reasonable data protection objection during that period. The parties will seek a solution; if none is available, the affected service may be ended with an appropriate refund of unused prepaid fees.

Cross-border processing is subject to applicable law and the approved location schedule. No unrestricted worldwide processing permission is created by this clause.

6. Assistance and Incidents

Fintle will reasonably assist with individual requests, legally required assessments and regulator enquiries relating to the covered processing. Requests received directly will be referred to the Customer where appropriate, without preventing Fintle from meeting its own duties.

Fintle will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal information breach affecting covered data, and no later than [[AGREED INCIDENT NOTICE DEADLINE]]. Initial notice may be supplemented as facts become available. It will include available details about affected information, likely consequences, response measures and a contact.

Both parties will cooperate to contain and investigate the incident. Each remains responsible for notifications imposed on it by law; a contractual notice period must not delay a shorter statutory obligation.

7. Return, Deletion and Evidence

At service end or on a valid instruction, Fintle will provide the agreed export opportunity and delete or return covered information according to [[RETENTION AND DELETION SCHEDULE]]. Legally retained records will be isolated from ordinary use. Backup copies will expire under the stated cycle and deletion will be reapplied after restoration.

Fintle will provide reasonable information to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum. If that is insufficient, the parties may arrange a proportionate confidential audit on reasonable notice, protecting other customers and system security. Statutory regulator powers are unaffected.

This Addendum prevails over conflicting general terms concerning covered processing. No term removes duties or individual rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. The liability and dispute provisions in the main agreement apply subject to those limits.
